    📖 How to Play

    👆

    1. Swipe or Flick

    Swipe up on screen to aim and throw. Watch the power ring to control throw strength!

    💧

    2. Water Weight

    Adjust water fill (0%–100%). More water lowers the centre of mass, making it easier to land upright!

    🎯

    3. Target Bullseye

    Land upright on the cyan bullseye for +150 PTS bonus. At D1+ the target starts moving!

    🔥

    4. Build Streaks

    Chain flips for a score multiplier (up to 5×). At streak 3+ you'll see the fire aura!

    🌬️

    5. Difficulty Scales

    Every 5 landings the table narrows, wind appears, and the target moves faster. Bounce off walls for trick shots!

    Motion Controls

    Physically flick your phone into the air to flip the bottle!

    Requires accelerometer sensor access on mobile devices.
